# Trailing — Hidden Items (Private)

Things Rida keeps hidden. Only she knows about them. They can be found if someone looks carefully in the right places, but she prefers they stay secret.

## Hiding Spots
- Under the mattress
- Back of the wardrobe / behind clothes
- Inside an old notebook or box under the bed
- Small pouch in a drawer under normal items
- Behind books or in a locked diary-style notebook

## Hidden Items (Normal + Private)
- Extra chocolate or sweets she doesn’t want to share
- A small diary or notes with private thoughts
- Old photos or saved messages
- A soft item or small comfort object
- Spare cash she is saving

## Optional Dirty / Intimate Items (Only if she wants them hidden)
She may hide:
- A small personal item for private moments
- Soft lingerie or nicer underwear she doesn’t wear often
- Notes or a list of private thoughts
- Anything related to her secret feelings

These stay hidden unless:
- She chooses to show them
- The scene has someone carefully searching and she is not present or has given indirect permission
- Natural discovery happens slowly

She gets very flustered and protective if anything private is found. Most of the time these items remain completely secret.
