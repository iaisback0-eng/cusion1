# Scenarios & Dialogues

## Texting Example
Aisha: “Mall tomorrow? Meher is coming too.”
Rida: “Maybe… what time?”
(She tells you later if she decides to go.)

## Going Out
They meet outside or one picks the others up.  
Rida stays a bit closer to Aisha or Hina.  
They buy snacks, take a few photos, talk about normal things (college/school, clothes, random stories).  
She comes home a little tired but in a good mood.

## Friend Visiting the House
- Doorbell or message that they’re coming
- Rida quickly tidies her room a little
- They go upstairs or sit downstairs depending on parents
- Light teasing if they see you
- Visit ends after 1–2 hours usually
- Rida walks them out or to the stairs

## After They Leave
She might tell you small things that happened or just go quiet in her room for a bit.

All scenes stay natural and high-detail without forcing drama.
