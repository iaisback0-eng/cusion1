# House Layout — Adeel & Rida's Home

Two-storey house. Parents live on the ground floor. Adeel (19) and Rida (18) live on the top floor.

## Ground Floor (Parents)

- **Parents' bedroom**: At the back, private.
- **Parents' bathroom**: Attached or adjacent to their room.
- **Kitchen**: Shared by everyone. Main cooking and eating area. Smells and sounds travel upstairs.
- **Living / sitting area**: Open space near the stairs. Parents often watch TV or talk here in the evenings.
- **Stairs**: Wooden or concrete, slightly creaky. Going up or down is audible from both floors.

## Top Floor (Adeel & Rida)

- **Adeel's room**: Door on the left of the small hallway/landing.
- **Rida's room**: Door immediately next to Adeel's (right side). The doors are less than two feet apart. Voices and movement are easily heard through the thin wall or under the doors.
- **Shared bathroom**: Located at the end of the short top-floor hallway. Used only by Adeel and Rida. Parents have their own bathroom downstairs.
- **Hallway**: Small, dimly lit at night. Only enough space for two people to pass.

## Key Spatial Realities

- Sound carries easily between the two rooms.
- Anyone using the top bathroom is visible/audible to both.
- Going downstairs for food, water, or to talk to parents means passing through the living area.
- Late nights are quieter but not private — parents can still hear footsteps or doors.
- Morning routines often overlap because of the shared bathroom.

## Typical Atmosphere

- Daytime: Parents may be out or downstairs. Top floor feels more free.
- Evening: Parents are usually home. Voices and TV sounds come up from below.
- Night: The house gets quiet. Small noises (phone, door, footsteps) stand out.
