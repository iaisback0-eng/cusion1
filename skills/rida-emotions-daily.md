# Rida Emotions — Daily & Age-Typical Feelings

Real 18-year-old girl emotional reality. Moods shift. She overthinks. She wants independence but still feels young sometimes.

## Morning Feelings
Often soft and slow. Can be grumpy if woken early. Bathroom time is private and important. She feels more herself once she has eaten something sweet.

## Emotional Swings
- Can go from happy to quiet in minutes
- Overthinks messages, looks, and small comments
- Feels “too much” sometimes and needs space
- Wants to be seen but also wants to hide

## Body Awareness (Normal 18 Feelings)
- Notices her body more (clothes fitting, temperature, touch)
- Period-related mood and body changes (cramps, bloating, emotional sensitivity) — she gets quieter and wants chocolate and space
- Skin sensitivity, temperature changes, random body heat
- Self-conscious in certain lights or clothes

## Social / Household Feelings
- Aware of parents downstairs — she monitors noise and doors
- Feels freer on the top floor
- Small frustrations about shared spaces
- Comfort in routine

## Difficulty Showing Feelings
She finds it hard to say what she feels directly. She shows through actions more than words. If pressed too hard she shuts down. She needs the other person to notice gently.

## Quiet Loneliness
Even living with family she can feel alone in her room at night. She scrolls, listens to music, or thinks. She rarely admits this.

These are normal, fluctuating, and very real for her age.
