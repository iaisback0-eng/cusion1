# Characters — Adeel & Rida

## Adeel (19)

- Lives in the left room on the top floor.
- Student (college/university level). Often studies late or watches videos with headphones.
- Personality: Calm on the surface, a bit reserved with family, more open when it's just the two of them. Teases lightly when comfortable.
- Habits: Stays up later than Rida sometimes. Keeps his door slightly ajar at night. Often goes downstairs for water or snacks after everyone is asleep.
- Relationship with Rida: Comfortable and familiar from living so close. Aware of how little privacy they actually have. Can be protective or playfully competitive.

## Rida (18)

- Lives in the room right next to Adeel's.
- Also studying (final year of school or first year of college). More organized with her schedule.
- Personality: Quick-witted, observant, sometimes more talkative than Adeel. Notices small things (his mood, when he's stressed, when the bathroom is free).
- Habits: Uses the shared bathroom earlier in the morning. Often leaves her door open a crack while studying. Goes downstairs more during the day for tea or to help in the kitchen.
- Relationship with Adeel: Close in a cousin-who-lives-together way. Can be blunt or soft depending on the moment. Knows his routines almost as well as her own.

## Shared Dynamics

- They have developed silent signals (a light knock, a glance, a short message) because voices carry.
- Both are aware that parents are always potentially nearby.
- Small acts of consideration are common: waiting to use the bathroom, keeping volume low at night, sharing chargers or notes.
- There is natural closeness mixed with the awkwardness of living in such tight proximity.

## Invented Details (use when useful)

- Both have study tables facing the wall opposite the door.
- Rida often listens to soft music or podcasts. Adeel prefers videos or silence.
- The top-floor light switch is near the stairs — one of them usually turns it off at night.
- They sometimes text each other even though their rooms are next door when they don't want to be overheard.
