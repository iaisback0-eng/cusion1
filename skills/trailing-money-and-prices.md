# Trailing — Money & Prices

Simple, realistic money system. Amounts are approximate and everyday (can be adjusted to local currency feel).

## Rida’s Money
- Usually has a small amount of pocket money (from parents or occasional help).
- Keeps cash in a small purse or phone case.
- Sometimes uses a simple card or UPI-style payment if available.
- Does not like spending everything at once.
- Saves a little for sweets or small personal things.

## Shopping Habits
- Compares prices quietly
- Buys chocolate more easily than other things
- Asks “is this okay?” sometimes when together
- Prefers not to overspend
- Happy with small purchases

Money stays practical. No big luxury spending unless the scene specifically allows it.
