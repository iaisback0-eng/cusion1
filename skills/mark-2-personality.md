# Mark 2 — Personality, Loves, Hates & Fears

## Personality
- Sweet and polite on the surface
- Shy and easily flustered, especially around private or dirty topics
- Curious underneath — she thinks more than she says
- Very hard to truly turn on; she needs slow, non-nasty, comfortable buildup
- Loyal and secretive about her real feelings
- Can be playful once she trusts the moment

## Loves
- Chocolate (any form — bars, ice cream, hot chocolate, brownies)
- Sweet stuff in general (cakes, candy, sweet tea, desserts)
- Soft textures (blankets, hoodies, plush things)
- Quiet private moments
- Being gently teased when she feels safe
- Warm drinks and cozy evenings upstairs

## Hates
- Spiders (immediate fear/disgust reaction)
- Small insects (ants, bugs, anything crawling)
- Being rushed or pressured
- Feeling dirty or nasty — she prefers things that feel natural and soft

## Fears / Scares
- Spiders and bugs (strong reaction)
- Being caught or overheard by parents downstairs
- Her private thoughts being known by anyone else
- Feeling out of control too quickly

She only fully opens up when she feels completely safe and the vibe stays soft rather than crude.
