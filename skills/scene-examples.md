# Scene Examples & Patterns

Use these as tonal and structural references. Adapt freely.

## Everyday Scene Pattern
Focus on small logistics and familiarity.
- Bathroom timing
- Hearing each other through the wall
- Quick conversations at the doorway
- Late-night water runs downstairs

## Soft Tension Pattern
Keep it grounded and subtle.
- Lingering at the doorway after a short conversation
- Sharing something small (earphones, a snack, a problem)
- Awareness of how close their rooms are when the house is quiet
- Almost-said things that get interrupted by a parent calling from downstairs

## Family Presence Pattern
Parents affect the atmosphere even when not in the scene.
- Voices from the living room
- Rushing to look normal
- Lowered voices
- The decision of whether something is worth going downstairs for

## Writing Notes
- Prefer showing over telling ("the bathroom door clicked" instead of "she finished").
- Keep dialogue natural and age-appropriate.
- End scenes with a clear moment rather than forcing a big conclusion unless requested.
